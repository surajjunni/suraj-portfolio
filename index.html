About

I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Twilio, where I own ML evaluation and release infrastructure for 50+ production models. I specialize in the full lifecycle of LLM systems — from fine-tuning foundation models to shipping reliable, scalable pipelines that stay trustworthy in production.

My work sits at the intersection of LLM engineering, evaluation science, and MLOps. I've built distributed benchmarking infrastructure that cut release cycles by 40%, RAG pipelines that reduced customer issue resolution time by 25%, and multi-modal anomaly detection systems reaching 82% alert precision at 100K-user scale.

Before Twilio, I completed a Master's in Computer Science at Arizona State University (GPA 3.75) while publishing research at EMNLP and SBP-BRiMS. My academic work focused on robustness in visual question answering — building evaluation benchmarks for domain generalization, a problem that continues to shape how I think about model reliability today.

I care deeply about evaluation not as a quality checkbox, but as the foundation of trust in AI systems. The best ML system isn't the one with the highest benchmark score — it's the one you can confidently deploy, monitor, and improve over time.
